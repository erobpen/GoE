# Former EduQuest product vision — superseded

EduQuest was the previous working name. The product is now **Gamification Go**, for education.

The current source of truth is **[Gamification_Go_Specification.md](Gamification_Go_Specification.md)**, based on the product owner's explanations and clarifications on 2026-11-01. It replaces the former SAFe product vision at this path.

In particular, use the current specification for separate teacher and content creator roles, authenticated gameplay, parental approvals, the common 3–10 monster configuration, per-level question requirements, video publishing, free-access requirements, and question-based combat without a second standard-combat phase.

The former technology stack, paid-access assumptions, mandatory AI features, and deferred parental controls are not approved requirements. Open decisions are listed in the current specification and must not be filled from the old proposal.

The original [goe.odt](goe.odt) is retained as historical material only, as explained in the [README](README.md).
